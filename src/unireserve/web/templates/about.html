About UniReserve

A simpler way to book campus study spaces.

How reservations work

Choose a room or individual workstation, select a date and time, and confirm availability. Some locations require administrator approval. A pending request is not a guaranteed reservation.

Availability indicators reflect confirmed reservations and configured opening hours. They do not measure whether someone is physically sitting at a desk.

Your booking is checked again when you confirm it or an administrator approves it.

Space information

Room names, opening schedules and reservation permissions are maintained within this installation. This independent university-inspired project is not an official AzTU service. Room imagery and seating layouts are designed representations, not verified campus floor surveys.

For verified access rules, room changes or account assistance, contact the space administrator.
